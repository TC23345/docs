## Business terms (new ground for Rylee)

- **PLLC (Illinois)** — Professional Limited Liability Company; the entity form used by licensed professionals in IL. BCBAs are licensed under the IL Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR); whether a PLLC is required (vs. a standard LLC) depends on IDFPR's current position on the BACB credential. Verify with IL SoS and IDFPR at filing time — do not infer.
- **Registered agent** — the person or service designated to receive legal mail on behalf of the entity. Required for LLC/PLLC filings.
- **EIN** — federal Employer Identification Number, obtained free from the IRS once the entity is registered with the state.
