# Scope of practice

This harness produces working drafts, checklists, and decision support for Rylee Coteus, M.S., BCBA, to use in building and running an IEP advocacy practice in Illinois. It is **not** legal, tax, insurance, or psychological advice, and outputs are not a substitute for licensed professional review.

## What this harness produces

- Brainstorming, drafts, and structured templates
- Checklists for the user (or a licensed professional) to verify against current law and policy
- Procedural walkthroughs of publicly documented processes (state filings, IEP timelines, IDEA procedural safeguards)
- Comparison frameworks for the user to weigh options

## What this harness does not produce

- **Entity tax classification** (S-corp vs LLC default treatment, etc.) — refer to a CPA licensed in IL
- **Binding contract terms or signed services agreements** — refer to an attorney licensed in IL
- **Specific legal strategy for a named child's IEP dispute, complaint, or due process case** — Rylee operates as the advocate; for legal representation, refer the family to a special-education attorney
- **Insurance coverage selection** — refer to a licensed insurance broker familiar with private practice professional liability
- **Clinical recommendations for a specific child** outside Rylee's own BCBA scope of practice
- **Current ISBE forms or filings** without verification against the live ISBE site — always confirm against `isbe.net` at time of use

## Invocation rule

Skills invoke this disclaimer **only when crossing one of the lines above**. Do not prefix every reply with a blanket disclaimer; that's noise. When you do invoke it, state which line was crossed and what professional should be consulted, then continue with the non-regulated portion of the work.
