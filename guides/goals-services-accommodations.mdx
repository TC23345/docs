---
title: "Goals, Services, and Accommodations"
description: "What to look for in the working parts of an IEP."
---

The working parts of an IEP should connect to each other. The present levels describe need, the goals identify what the student is working toward, and the services and accommodations describe support.

## The connection to check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Present levels name the need">
    The plan should describe how the disability affects school participation and progress in a way the team can act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Goals match the need">
    Annual goals should be measurable and connected to the student's academic or functional needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services support the goals">
    Services should be specific enough to understand what is being provided, how often, where, and for how long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accommodations remove barriers">
    Accommodations should be written clearly enough that staff can implement them consistently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

| IEP section | Parent question |
| --- | --- |
| Present levels | Does this describe the real concern clearly? |
| Annual goals | Can progress be measured? |
| Services | Are frequency, duration, location, and provider role clear enough? |
| Accommodations | Would a new teacher understand what to do? |
| Progress reports | When and how will the family know whether the plan is working? |

## When something feels vague

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ask for the data">
    What evaluation, progress report, observation, service log, or work sample supports this section?
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask how progress will be measured">
    What will be counted, observed, scored, or reported? How often will the parent receive updates?
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask who is responsible">
    Who provides the service, implements the accommodation, tracks progress, and communicates with the family?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
