---
title: "Discipline and MDR Basics"
description: "How to organize records around school discipline concerns."
---

Discipline concerns can move quickly. Start by organizing the timeline and the documents. If there are legal deadlines, serious placement issues, police involvement, expulsion risk, or a formal hearing, contact a qualified attorney.

<Warning>
  Discipline and placement disputes can become legal matters quickly. Shirley Road can help organize records and questions, but legal strategy belongs with a qualified attorney.
</Warning>

## First records to gather

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Incident and removal records" icon="file-warning" horizontal>
    Incident reports, suspension notices, removal dates, and any discipline letters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Current support plan" icon="file-text" horizontal>
    Current IEP or 504 plan, behavior intervention plan, accommodations, and service page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evaluation and behavior data" icon="clipboard-list" horizontal>
    Evaluations, functional behavior assessment if available, behavior data, and teacher observations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Communication timeline" icon="mails" horizontal>
    Emails, call notes, meeting notices, and what happened before and after the incident.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Questions to ask quickly

<Steps>
  <Step title="What exactly is the school proposing?">
    Ask whether the school is proposing suspension, removal, alternative setting, manifestation determination review, expulsion referral, or a placement change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How many school days has the student been removed?">
    Track cumulative and consecutive days. Under IDEA discipline rules, removal length and change-of-placement questions matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is an MDR being discussed?">
    For certain disciplinary changes in placement, the LEA, parent, and relevant IEP team members review relevant information to determine whether the conduct was caused by or had a direct and substantial relationship to the disability, or was the direct result of failure to implement the IEP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What supports were in place before the incident?">
    Review whether the IEP, 504 plan, behavior plan, accommodations, or services were being implemented.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to seek legal advice

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The school mentions expulsion, hearing, or alternative placement">
    These are legal-risk signals. Contact an attorney or legal aid resource promptly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The family disagrees with the MDR outcome">
    Ask for written documentation and consider legal consultation about options and deadlines.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The situation involves safety, restraint, seclusion, police, or serious injury">
    These concerns may require legal, clinical, or other specialized support beyond educational advocacy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  Official starting point: [IDEA discipline regulation Sec. 300.530](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/e/300.530).
</Info>
