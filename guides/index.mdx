These guides are written for parents who need a calmer way into the special education process. They are not a substitute for legal advice or a full review of a child's records.

<Info>
  Use the guides as a starting point. If a concern involves deadlines, discipline, placement disputes, due process, or legal strategy, speak with a qualified attorney.
</Info>

## A records-first reading order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the current plan">
    Read the current IEP or 504 plan before older records. It shows what the school has already agreed to provide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then read the evaluation">
    The evaluation explains the data the team used to identify needs, eligibility, services, and accommodations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then read progress and communication">
    Progress reports, notices, service logs, and emails show whether the plan is being implemented and whether the concern has already been documented.
  </Step>
</Steps>
