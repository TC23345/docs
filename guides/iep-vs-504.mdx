---
title: "IEP vs. 504"
description: "A parent-facing comparison of IEPs and 504 plans."
---

IEPs and 504 plans both support students with disabilities, but they are not the same tool. An IEP is tied to special education under IDEA. A 504 plan is tied to disability access and nondiscrimination under Section 504.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="IEP">
    An Individualized Education Program is used when a student qualifies for special education services. It should describe present levels, measurable annual goals, special education and related services, accommodations, placement, and progress reporting.

    **Common parent question:** What specialized instruction or related services does my child need to make progress?
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="504 plan">
    A 504 plan is used for disability-related access and accommodations. It may support a student who needs accommodations but does not qualify for special education services.

    **Common parent question:** What accommodations does my child need to access school as fully as peers?
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Practical comparison

| Question | IEP | 504 plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main purpose | Special education and related services | Access and accommodations |
| Common law reference | IDEA | Section 504 |
| Usually includes goals | Yes | Not usually |
| Usually includes specialized instruction | Yes, if needed | No |
| Parent prep focus | Needs, goals, services, placement, progress | Barriers, accommodations, implementation |

## Questions to ask the team

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What need is the school trying to address?">
    Ask the team to name the academic, functional, behavioral, communication, health, or access need in plain language.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has the student been evaluated recently?">
    Current evaluation data often drives eligibility, services, goals, accommodations, and placement conversations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How will progress or effectiveness be monitored?">
    For an IEP, ask how progress toward goals will be measured. For a 504 plan, ask how the team will know the accommodations are being implemented and helping.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
