Shirley Road IEP Advocacy is being built as a calm, practical support for families navigating IEP and 504 conversations in Illinois.

The practice is grounded in records, preparation, and clear next steps. The goal is to help parents understand the paperwork, organize their concerns, and participate more confidently in school meetings.

## BCBA-led perspective

Rylee's background as a board-certified behavior analyst gives her a careful eye for data, observation, goals, services, behavior supports, and how school teams talk through student support.

That experience can be useful in special education advocacy, but the public-facing service line remains clear: Shirley Road provides advocacy and educational support, not ABA therapy, legal representation, or clinical diagnosis.

## A calm planning style

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the concern">
    What is happening, what decision is coming up, and what does the parent need to understand?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the records">
    What do the IEP, 504 plan, evaluations, progress data, notices, and emails actually show?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the conversation">
    What questions should be asked, what records should be referenced, and what follow-up should be documented?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clarify the next step">
    What should happen next, who owns it, and when should the family follow up?
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Shirley Road IEP Advocacy provides advocacy, preparation, and educational support for families. It does not provide legal advice, legal representation, clinical diagnosis, therapy services, or guaranteed school outcomes.
</Warning>
