## Clear records. Clear priorities. Clear next steps.

Shirley Road IEP Advocacy helps Illinois families prepare for IEP and 504 conversations with practical records review, meeting preparation, and parent-centered advocacy support.

<Info>
  If a meeting is already scheduled, start by gathering the current plan, the most recent evaluation, progress notes, school emails, and the meeting notice. The first goal is not to read everything at once. The first goal is to find the decision the team is about to make.
</Info>

## A simple first pass

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the decision">
    Is the school team discussing eligibility, services, placement, behavior, accommodations, evaluation, transition, or follow-up from a prior meeting?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull the records">
    Start with the current IEP or 504 plan, evaluation reports, progress data, prior written notices, meeting notes, and recent school communication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write three parent priorities">
    Keep the list concrete: what is not working, what record shows it, and what question needs to be answered next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether advocacy is enough">
    Some concerns need educational advocacy and preparation. Some need a special education attorney, clinical provider, or another licensed professional.
  </Step>
</Steps>
