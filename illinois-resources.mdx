</Info>

## When to escalate

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use public resources for official information">
    ISBE and parent centers are good starting points for state guidance, parent rights information, sample letters, records organization, and training.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use legal resources for legal-risk situations">
    Due process, formal complaints, discipline hearings, expulsion, discrimination, placement disputes, and legal deadlines should be reviewed with a qualified legal resource.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use advocacy support for preparation">
    Shirley Road can help organize records, clarify questions, prepare for meetings, and identify when another professional should be involved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
